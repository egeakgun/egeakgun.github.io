What I worked on

I'm doing research under Prof. Olga Holtz, working with Marco David and Dan Romik on reinforcement learning for the Four Color Theorem.

I'm investigating a two-player game formulation of D-reducibility to understand the structure of computer-assisted proofs and explore simpler proof certificates using machine learning.

The question

Can a two-player game formulation of D-reducibility help uncover structure in computer-assisted proofs and support simpler proof certificates?

Where it stands

This work is ongoing. I started in September 2026.
