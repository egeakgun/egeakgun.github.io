Leakage-controlled portfolio research

An eight-asset forecasting and backtesting framework with chronological validation and transaction costs.

OrganizationIndependent project
RoleIndependent researcher & developer
Period2026
8crypto assets
1,461daily observations

My contribution

Built a leakage-controlled cryptocurrency forecasting and backtesting framework across eight assets using PyTorch LSTMs, logistic regression, and constrained mean-variance optimization.

Evaluated strategies out of sample under transaction costs using Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, and drawdown metrics.

The challenge: keeping the test genuinely out of sample

Chronological train, validation, and test splits separate fitting from model selection and final evaluation. Scalers are fitted on training observations; hyperparameters and decision thresholds are selected on validation data. Position changes incur fees and slippage.

Results & scope

In the reference test, the LSTM had a smaller maximum drawdown than BTC buy-and-hold, but lower return and Sharpe. This is one test regime; it does not establish consistent outperformance.

Limitations

Daily close-to-close data and a single market regime limit the conclusions. Short borrow costs, market impact, funding rates, and tax effects are not modeled. Eight assets introduce universe-selection risk.
