Crypto forecasting & backtesting

Independent project · 2026

An eight-asset forecasting and backtesting framework with chronological validation and transaction costs.

Independent researcher & developer

What I worked on

I built a cryptocurrency forecasting and backtesting framework across eight assets, using PyTorch LSTMs, logistic regression, and constrained mean-variance optimization.

I separated training, validation, and testing chronologically and evaluated strategies out of sample after transaction costs. The reports include Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, and drawdown metrics.

Keeping the test out of sample

I used chronological splits to separate fitting, model selection, and final evaluation. Scalers are fitted on training observations. Hyperparameters and decision thresholds are chosen on validation data. Position changes incur fees and slippage.

Results & scope

In the reference test, the LSTM had a smaller maximum drawdown than BTC buy-and-hold, but lower return and Sharpe. This is one test regime; it does not establish consistent outperformance.

Reference test: August 2024–July 2025. Results reproduced in the linked repository.

Limitations

Daily close-to-close data and a single market regime limit the conclusions. Short borrow costs, market impact, funding rates, and tax effects are not modeled. Eight assets introduce universe-selection risk.
