Computing on encrypted data

TÜBİTAK BİLGEM · 2022 — 2025

A Legendre-symbol-based partially homomorphic encryption prototype in Python and SageMath.

Cryptography Research Intern

What I worked on

I designed and prototyped a Legendre-symbol-based partially homomorphic encryption scheme in Python and SageMath for computation on encrypted healthcare data.

I joined at 14 as one of three selected high-school researchers. During the three-year internship, I studied graduate-level cryptography and worked with senior cryptographers.

From a cryptographic idea to a prototype

The research question was how to support computation on encrypted healthcare data with a partially homomorphic encryption scheme. My work connected the mathematical construction to a working prototype.

Results & scope

I built a research prototype in Python and SageMath.
